Meet Panchayat.
A team of AI agents that takes a household's complaint to the right office, and stays on the case until it is resolved.
Built on Strands Agents and Amazon Bedrock AgentCore.
Here it is, live.
A resident writes what's wrong, in their own words.
The Intake agent reads it back first, because a misheard complaint, chased for eleven weeks, helps no one.
The Household agent brings the family's view together, so a grandmother's dialysis on Thursday makes the case urgent, even if nobody typed it.
The Privacy Warden decides what leaves the home. The urgency goes out. The medical reason stays private.
Remedy finds which office is responsible, from a verified jurisdiction table, and cites the rule. It never guesses.
Nothing is filed until a real person signs. The Digest agent asks one member of the household, in their language, and waits.
Once it's signed, the filing travels, agent to agent, to a simulated water board desk that keeps its own records.
Desks refuse, or go quiet, just like real offices. Panchayat holds the clock and tries again, until a ticket number comes back.
Now comes the part no resident has time for.
The Watchdog holds the legal clock. If the office misses its seven day deadline, it prepares the escalation to the next officer, and then the next, and a person signs each step.
Pattern Watch listens quietly. When neighbours on the same water line report the same fault, it joins them into one stronger case.
Anti Abuse checks that every neighbour is real, has consented, and lives on that line, so the pressure stays honest.
And if an office marks the case resolved while the taps are still dry, the street's own reports dispute it, and the case climbs again.
